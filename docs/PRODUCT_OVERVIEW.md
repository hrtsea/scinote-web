## 二、五大核心模块（SCINOTE ELN FUNCTIONALITIES）

### 1. DATA MANAGEMENT
- **Hierarchical file structure** — Organize your data in a structured way (projects/experiments/tasks) and customize SciNote based on your lab's processes.
- **Interactive protocols & centralized protocol repository** — Manage protocols in the centralized protocol repository.
- **Advanced search** — Use the advanced search to quickly locate the data you are looking for.
- **Smart annotation & activity log** — Cross-reference people, inventory items and tasks with smart annotation to ensure full traceability. Track all activities within SciNote through the activity log.

### 2. PROJECT MANAGEMENT
- **Follow visualized workflows** — Create visualized workflows (linear or non-linear) in the project canvas to show how steps are connected.
- **Template repeatable tasks** — Create templates with repeatable tasks and workflows.
- **Assign tasks and set up reminders** — Assign tasks to lab members, set due dates to tasks, mark completed tasks.
- **Task monitoring** — Monitor task progress and deadlines on the SciNote dashboard.
- **Report generator** — Generate reports for routine review and discussions.

### 3. INVENTORY MANAGEMENT
- **Stock management & reminder** — Manage what you use in the lab – from reagents, samples, to supplies and equipment. Track usage and receive alerts for low stock or scheduled activities.
- **Link your inventory to experiments** — Ensure full traceability by linking reagents, protocols, to experiments and results. Share inventories between members of the team or multiple teams.
- **Barcoding & custom labels** — Keep your lab organized with barcodes and custom labels.
- **Import/export your data** — Import existing Excel inventories into SciNote. Or export your SciNote inventories and stock consumption into a spreadsheet.

### 4. REGULATORY COMPLIANCE
- **Closed system with restricted access** — Assured by secured system-login, unique for each individual SciNote user.
- **Human readable copies** — Create human readable copies of your digital data at any time; the full export function exports all electronic data in a human readable format with all attachments neatly organized in folders.
- **Time-stamped audit trails** — Audit trails independently record the date and time of each user's entries and actions. The audit trails cannot be edited or deleted.
- **Time-stamped electronic signatures** — Unique to one individual and indisputably linked to the respective electronic record to prevent fraudulent use.

### 5. TEAM MANAGEMENT & COLLABORATION
- **Collaborate anytime from anywhere** — Co-located, distributed, or fully remote teams can work together at any time.
- **Improve cross-team communication and collaboration** — Real-time communication via tagging colleagues, posting comments, following notifications.
- **Customize user permissions** — Establish custom team sharing policies and user permissions according to internal and external collaboration.
- **Onboard your team in no time** — Personalized onboarding experience; dedicated Customer Success Manager (CSM) per plan.

## 四、FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS（FAQ 全文原文）

**Q1: How is SciNote different from other ELN or data management software?**
A1: Here are a few things our users mentioned, that set us apart from other ELN or data management platforms:
- A visualized workflow that will help you plan experiments and ensure consistency.
- Smart annotations allowing you to refer to other components within SciNote – may it be inventory items, protocols, projects, experiments, or team members.
- The flexible structure that you can customize to best suit your lab workflows. This is also why you can grow and scale quickly with SciNote while maintaining a wealth of features and functionalities.
- A well-structured API to allow granularity in connections. We also offer all instrument/app connectivity through our Ganymede integration.
- An integrated inventory management system that can automate many aspects of inventory tracking and ensure inventory traceability.
- A dedicated Customer Success Manager (CSM) with onboarding and training all included within Premium Plans without any additional cost.

**Q2: Can I connect SciNote with other software or instruments?**
A2: Yes! We offer RESTful API solutions. We are happy to share additional documentations with your IT personnel so you can determine what is feasible. We have several SciNote users using APIs to connect SciNote with other software and instruments; here are a few examples of how SciNote users use our API to connect SciNote to other software and instruments. You can also watch this webinar to learn more.

SciNote comes with a few out-of-box integrations, such as Microsoft Office (Word, Excel, PowerPoint), Protocols.io, and label makers including FLUICS Print and selected Zebra label printers. For a complete list of available integrations, visit our integrations page.

SciNote is now partnering with Ganymede to offer all instrument/app connectivity, so you can get a holistic live view of all of your data, ensure data integrity and traceability, while speeding up R&D, compliance, analysis, and decision-making. Learn more about the SciNote-Ganymede integration.

**Q3: We are starting to evaluate ELN and data management options. Do you have resources I could use?**
A3: For ELN evaluation, check out our downloadable resources page. We highly recommend the ELN selection template that can help you systemically evaluate different ELN options.

You can speed up your research process by emailing our team at premium@scinote.net or booking a short meeting with our specialists and have all your ELN questions answered quickly.

**Q4: Why should I use SciNote if I already use a digital notebook like OneNote and Evernote?**
A4: SciNote is specifically designed for lab use, with a unique project/experiment/task structure that can help you plan and manage your research protocols, notes, and data. It offers access control, activity tracking, collaboration tools, and has other lab management and inventory management features that are not available in digital notebooks such as OneNote and Evernote.

You can learn more about the differences in our blog post Data management and sharing: Paper vs OneNote vs SciNote [comparison].

**Q5: Can I import my inventories into SciNote? What if I use Quartzy to manage my inventory?**
A5: You can import your inventory spreadsheets into SciNote's inventory management system. You can also import your inventories from another inventory management platform, as long as the platform allows you to export your existing data in the .csv format. Lastly, you can sync between inventory databases and create automatic uploads into SciNote's inventory management system using SciNote API. Learn more about how you can import inventories.

SciNote will be rolling out its Quartzy integration in 2024.

**Q6: What security requirements do you meet?**
A6: SciNote is currently in the FedRAMP Authorization process as a part of our commitment to customer needs and the trust they place in our product. The Federal Risk and Authorization Management Program (FedRAMP®) is a United States federal government-wide compliance program that provides a standardized approach to security assessment, authorization, and continuous monitoring for cloud products and services. It provides a standardized approach to security authorizations for Cloud Service Offerings.

Our security standards align with and go beyond the requirements of many security certifications. Upon request, we will provide supporting documents to demonstrate our information security management system (ISMS), as defined by ISO/IEC 27001.

SciNote is used at the FDA, USDA and other industry organizations. Learn more about the steps we take toward data protection in our Data Protection Whitepaper.

If you have any additional questions regarding our data protection and certification progress, please contact us at premium@scinote.net.

**Q7: What kind of onboarding, customer support, and training does SciNote offer? Is there additional cost to onboarding and training?**
A7: There is no additional cost for onboarding and training.

We will work closely with you to set up your account, provide onboarding and training, and review your plan to ensure SciNote is helping you achieve your business goals.

We provide training materials on YouTube and through our web knowledge base and SciNote courses; you can use these materials to train additional lab members. We also maintain a LinkedIn SciNote community where users can ask questions and exchange tips to learn from each other.

Finally, each SciNote plan comes with a dedicated Customer Success Manager (CSM), so you can reach out to your CSM whenever you have any questions or require support.
